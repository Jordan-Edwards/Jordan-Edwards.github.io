What is real about this mock
  • The screen is drawn at the device size, 480 by 220, from a fixed list of shapes in 16 colours. Shapes only move, hide, grow or swap colour. The device can do all of that.
  • The device makes no sound unless a note is sounding. The wind and drone live under your notes and their fade-outs, so here they also stop when the last note has died away.
  • The device cannot tell the synth when you let go of a key. Rift's sinking pitch therefore runs on a clock from the start of the note.
  • The room echo stands in for the device's own reverb send.
  • Dig here only cuts the number of loudness steps. The device will also cut the sample rate.
