Tracker Mini · looper concept 2

Four loops, sweeping over each other

Every lane has its own playhead running forward, backward or back and forth. Tap Middle to start, then pick a lane with the Fn keys.

Tap Middle. Sound starts with your first tap.

What changed from concept 1

No bars and no pixel blocks. Each lane is a smooth stereo outline, left channel above its centre line and right below, filled with a soft gradient.

All four playheads run at once and cross over each other. Each leaves a comet tail as long as its speed, on the side it came from, so direction reads at a glance.

The lane you pick grows; the others ease down. Nothing jumps.

Can the Mini draw this?

Mostly yes. Its display chip draws anti-aliased lines and filled strips with sub-pixel positions, alpha blending and gradients in hardware, so the jagged look of concept 1 was a choice, not a limit.

The tight part is the drawing budget, about 8 KB per frame. Four stereo outlines at 96 points per side is roughly 3 KB, which leaves room for tails, labels and keys. The glow is drawn as a few wide translucent lines, which the chip does cheaply.

What this is

A design mock in a web page, with four short loops made up in code. It is not firmware and has not run on the device. The key layout under the screen is a sketch.
