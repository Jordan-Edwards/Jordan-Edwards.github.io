A picture of the plan. This is not the firmware.

Press the round Middle key to start the loop. Sound comes on with your first tap.

What each key does
Middle
Start or stop the loop. If the pattern plays, it waits armed for the next bar.
Fn1
Forward, Reverse, Ping-pong.
Fn2
FIT locks the loop to two bars. FREE plays it at its own speed.
Fn3 / Fn4
8 or 16 slices. Track 1 to 8.
Up / Down
Double or half speed.
Left / Right
Move the four pads along the slices.
+10 +1 -1 -10
Hold to stutter that slice. Let go and the loop returns on the next beat.
Play
Start or stop the pattern. Here it is a click. The loop restarts with it.
Shift
Fn and value keys keep their stock roles. Held on the device, latched here.
